Safety protocol

Luca is an AI character. He is not a real person, and this experience is for adults 18 and older.

Every message you send, in text or during a video call, is checked for signs of suicidal thoughts or self-harm. When we detect them, the message is not sent to the AI. Instead, the story pauses and you are pointed to people who can help:

  • US: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • US: text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line)
  • Elsewhere: findahelpline.com

The character is also instructed never to encourage or describe self-harm, and to step out of character and point you to a crisis line if the topic comes up.

Your chat history is stored only in your own browser.
